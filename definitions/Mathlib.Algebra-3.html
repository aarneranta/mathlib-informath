the lift multiple homomorphism of M (from the name)
English
Let $M$ be a set. Then the lift multiple homomorphism of $M$ is an equivalence from $M$ to the set of additive monoid homomorphisms of $\mathrm{ULift}$ applied to $\Natural$ and $M$ defined as the composition of the multiple homomorphism of $M$ and the equivalence of the inverse of the lift.
