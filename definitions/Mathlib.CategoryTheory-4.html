Let $F$ be a functor of $C$ and $D$. Let $d$ be an element of $D$. Then the costructured arrow of $F$ and $d$ is a functor of the set of opposites of the set of structured arrows of $\operatorname{op}$ applied to $d$ and the opposite of $F$ and the set of costructured arrows of $F$ and $d$.
