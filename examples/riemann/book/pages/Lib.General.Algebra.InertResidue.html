235. Lib.General.Algebra.InertResidue

src/Lib/General/Algebra/InertResidue.lean

A general count of the fixed points of an involution on a finite field, applied to finite places of a stably unramified CM field: a place with w̄ = w has norm r ^ 2 with r ≡ 3 mod 4, so a place of prime norm is split.
Theorem 235.1src/Lib/General/Algebra/InertResidue.lean: card_selfAdjoint
Let $k$ be a set such that $\FieldMACRoBDG {k}$, $\StarRingMACRoEXA {k}$ and $k$ is finite. Let $i \in k$ such that $i \times i$ is equal to $\NegnegMACRoDTE {1}$ and $\StarstarMACRoIOO {i}\neq i$. Then $\NatcardMACRoDSR {(\SubtypeSet {x_{2}}{(\MembershipmemMACRoDLK {\selfAdjointMACRoHAC {k}}{x_{2}})})}^ {2}= \NatcardMACRoDSR {k}$ and $\HModhModMACRoDC {\NatcardMACRoDSR {(\SubtypeSet {x_{3}}{(\MembershipmemMACRoDLK {\selfAdjointMACRoHAC {k}}{x_{3}})})}}{4}= 3$.
A finite field with an involution that moves a square root of `-1` has `r ^ 2` elements, where `r`, the number of fixed points, is `3` modulo `4`.
Proof.
By 12 lemmas of Mathlib. ∎ FiniteField.isSquare_neg_one_iff, IsSelfAdjoint.add_star_self, star_neg, sq, Nat.card_congr, Iff.not_left, Nat.card_prod, selfAdjoint.mem_iff, StarMul.star_mul, star_inj, Fintype.card_eq_nat_card, star_one
Theorem 235.2src/Lib/General/Algebra/InertResidue.lean: StablyUnram.exists_sq_eq_q_of_inert
Let $E$ be a number field such that $\FieldMACRoBDG {E}$, $\NumberFieldIsCMFieldMACRoIMM {E}$ and $\StablyUnramMACRoEWU {E}$. Let $w$ be a finite place of $E$ such that $\StarstarMACRoIOO {w}$ is equal to $w$. Then there exists a natural number $r$, such that $r ^ {2}= \FinPlaceqMACRoBJD {w}$ and $\HModhModMACRoDC {r}{4}= 3$.
For stably unramified E and a finite place w with w = w̄, the norm of w is r² for a natural number r ≡ 3 mod 4.
Proof. By mem_star_place, card_selfAdjoint, StablyUnram, StablyUnram.exists_i_c₀, starRingOfInvolution
and 5 lemmas of Mathlib. ∎ Eq.ge, star_star, one_add_one_eq_two, Ideal.Quotient.eq_zero_iff_mem, Eq.trans_ne
