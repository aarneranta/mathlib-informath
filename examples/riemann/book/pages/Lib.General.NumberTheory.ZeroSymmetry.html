Theorem 32.1src/Lib/General/NumberTheory/ZeroSymmetry.lean: prod_euler_factor_ne_zero
Let $N , M \in \Natural$. Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character modulo $N$. Let $s$ be a complex such that $\ComplexreMACRoINP {s}\neq 0$. Then $\FinsetProd {\NatprimeFactorsMACRoDSZ {M}}{p}{(1 - \chi (p)\times p ^ {\NegnegMACRoDTE {s}})}\neq 0$.
For a Dirichlet character χ, a natural number M and Re s ≠ 0, the product of the Euler factors 1 − χ(p)p^{−s} over the primes p dividing M is not zero.
Proof.
By 7 lemmas of Mathlib. ∎ Complex.cpow_neg, Nat.Prime.pos, Finset.prod_ne_zero_iff, Complex.norm_natCast_cpow_of_pos, MulChar.star_apply', Nat.Prime.ne_one, MulChar.inv_apply_eq_inv'
Theorem 32.2src/Lib/General/NumberTheory/ZeroSymmetry.lean: exists_isPrimitive_changeLevel_eq
Let $N$ be a nonzero natural number. Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character modulo $N$. Then there exists a natural number $M$, such that there exists an element $f$ of $\divides {M}{N}$, such that there exists a Dirichlet character $\psi$ modulo $M$, such that $M$ is nonzero, $\psi$ is primitive and $\mathrm{DirichletCharacter\_\_changeLevel\_\_app} (f , \psi)$ is equal to $\chi$.
Every complex Dirichlet character χ mod N, with N ≥ 1, is induced by a primitive character ψ mod M for some divisor M ≥ 1 of N.
Proof.
By 3 lemmas of Mathlib. ∎ DirichletCharacter.primitiveCharacter_isPrimitive, DirichletCharacter.changeLevel_primitiveCharacter, DirichletCharacter.conductor_ne_zero
Theorem 32.3src/Lib/General/NumberTheory/ZeroSymmetry.lean: LFunction_one_sub_eq_zero
Let $N$ be a nonzero natural number. Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character modulo $N$. Let $s$ be a complex such that $\ComplexreMACRoINP {s}\neq 0$, $s \neq 1$ and $\DirichletCharacterLFunctionMACRoIMK {\chi}{s}$ is equal to $0$. Then the Gamma factor of $\chi$ and $s$ is equal to $0$ or $\DirichletCharacterLFunctionMACRoIMK {\InvinvMACRoCBV {\chi}}{(1 - s)}$ is equal to $0$.
Symmetry of the zeros: if L(s, χ) = 0, re s ≠ 0 and s ≠ 1, then either s is a pole of the gamma factor of χ (where Mathlib gives it the value 0), or L(1 − s, χ⁻¹) = 0.
Proof. By exists_isPrimitive_changeLevel_eq, prod_euler_factor_ne_zero
and 7 lemmas of Mathlib. ∎ map_inv, DirichletCharacter.LFunction_changeLevel, DirichletCharacter.IsPrimitive.completedLFunction_one_sub, DirichletCharacter.LFunction_eq_completed_div_gammaFactor, Complex.ext_iff, DirichletCharacter.conductor_inv, DirichletCharacter.changeLevel_eq_cast_of_dvd
