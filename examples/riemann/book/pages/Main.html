Theorem 794.1src/Main.lean: riemannHypothesis
Let $s$ be a complex such that $\riemannZetaMACRoIML {s}$ is equal to $0$, there exists no natural number $n$, such that $s$ is equal to $\NegnegMACRoDTE {2}\times (n + 1)$ and $s \neq 1$. Then $\ComplexreMACRoINP {s}= \frac{ 1}{2}$.
Proof. By New.grh, New.zeta_zeros_on_line. ∎

Theorem 794.3src/Main.lean: LFunction_ne_zero_of_half_lt_re
Let $N$ be a nonzero natural number. Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character modulo $N$. Let $s$ be a complex such that $\frac{ 1}{2}< \ComplexreMACRoINP {s}$ and either $\chi \neq 1$ or $s \neq 1$. Then $\DirichletCharacterLFunctionMACRoIMK {\chi}{s}\neq 0$.
Proof. By New.grh. ∎
Theorem 794.4src/Main.lean: LFunction_eq_zero_re

Proof. By New.grh, New.zeros_on_line. ∎
Theorem 794.5src/Main.lean: LFunction_eq_zero_re_of_isPrimitive
