Definition 110.2src/New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean: IndexDvd

Then $\IndexDvdMACRoBCR {M}{N}{z}$ is a proposition defined as the proposition that for all natural numbers $n$, for all functions $\Phi$ from $\FinMACRoFDI {n}$ to $V$, if for all elements $k_{1}$ of $\FinMACRoFDI {n}$, $\Phi (k_{1})$ is an element of $M$, then there exists a matrix $m$ of $\FinMACRoFDI {n}$ and $O$, such that the determinant of $m$ divides $z$ and for all elements $i$ of $\FinMACRoFDI {n}$, $\FinsetSum {\FinsetunivMACRoDZ}{k}{(\HSMulhSMulMACRoEI {m (i , k)}{\Phi (k)})}$ is an element of $N$.

Theorem 110.3src/New/Core/ModuleIndex.lean: IndexDvd.comap
Let $O$ be a commutative ring. Let $U$ and $V$ be additive commutative groups such that $U$ is a module over $O$ and $V$ is a module over $O$. Let $M$ and $N$ be submodules of $V$ over $O$. Let $M'$ and $N'$ be submodules of $U$ over $O$. Let $z \in O$ such that $\IndexDvdMACRoBCR {M}{N}{z}$. Let $f$ be a linear map of $U$ and $V$ such that $M'$ is less than or equal to the preimage of $f$ and $M$ and for all elements $x_{5}$ of $U$, if $x_{5}$ is an element of $M'$, then if $f (x_{5})$ is an element of $N$, then $x_{5}$ is an element of $N'$. `IndexDvd` pulls back along a linear map f: U → V: if it holds for (M, N, z), f(M') ⊆ M and every x in M' with f(x) in N lies in N', then it holds for (M', N', z).
Proof. By Lean's tactics, without named lemmas. ∎
