Definition 112.1src/New/Endgame/PrimeError.lean: lambdaClass
Let $d , M , \nu \in \Natural$. Then $\lambdaClassMACRoEDF {d}{M}{\nu}$ is a real number defined as the product of the residue class of $1$ and $\nu$ and $1 - \mathrm{chiK\_\_app} (d , \nu)$.
The weight λ(ν) = Λ(ν)(1 − (ν/d)) for ν ≡ 1 mod M, and 0 otherwise; Λ is von Mangoldt's function and (ν/d) the Legendre symbol.
Definition 112.2src/New/Endgame/PrimeError.lean: lambdaSum
Let $d , M , r \in \Natural$. Let $y \in \Real$. Then $\lambdaSumMACRoEDH {d}{M}{r}{y}$ is a real number defined as $\frac{ \FinsetSum {(\FinsetIccMACRoEE {1}{\mathrm{Nat\_\_floor} (y)})}{k}{(\frac{ \lambdaClassMACRoEDF {d}{M}{k}}{k}\times k ^ {r})}}{y ^ {r}}$.
The sum y^{−r} ∑_{1≤ν≤y} λ(ν) ν^{r−1}, with λ = `lambdaClass`: for r = 0 it is ∑ λ(ν)/ν, for r = 1 it is y⁻¹ ∑ λ(ν).

Definition 112.4src/New/Endgame/PrimeError.lean: primeError
