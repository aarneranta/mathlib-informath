Theorem 165.1src/Main.lean: riemannHypothesis
Let $s$ be a complex such that $\EqtMACRoDE {\riemannZetaMACRoFDB {s}}{0}$, there exists no natural number $n$, such that $\EqtMACRoDE {s}{\NegnegMACRoFV {2}\times (n + 1)}$ and $s \neq 1$. Then $\ComplexreMACRoFEF {s}= \frac{ 1}{2}$.
Proof. By New.grh, New.zeta_zeros_on_line. ∎
Theorem 165.2src/Main.lean: riemannZeta_eq_zero_re
Let $s$ be a complex such that $\EqtMACRoDE {\riemannZetaMACRoFDB {s}}{0}$, there exists no natural number $n$, such that $\EqtMACRoDE {s}{\NegnegMACRoFV {2}\times (n + 1)}$ and $s \neq 1$. Then $\ComplexreMACRoFEF {s}= \frac{ 1}{2}$.

Theorem 165.3src/Main.lean: LFunction_ne_zero_of_half_lt_re
Let $N$ be a nonzero natural number. Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character modulo $N$. Let $s$ be a complex such that $\frac{ 1}{2}< \ComplexreMACRoFEF {s}$ and either $\chi \neq 1$ or $s \neq 1$. Then $\DirichletCharacterLFunctionMACRoFDA {\chi}{s}\neq 0$.
Proof. By New.grh. ∎
Theorem 165.4src/Main.lean: LFunction_eq_zero_re
Let $N$ be a nonzero natural number. Let $\chi$ be a Dirichlet character modulo $N$. Let $s$ be a complex such that $0 < \ComplexreMACRoFEF {s}< 1$ and $\DirichletCharacterLFunctionMACRoFDA {\chi}{s}$ is equal to $0$. Then $\ComplexreMACRoFEF {s}= \frac{ 1}{2}$.
Proof. By New.grh, New.zeros_on_line. ∎
Theorem 165.5src/Main.lean: LFunction_eq_zero_re_of_isPrimitive

Proof. By LFunction_eq_zero_re. ∎
