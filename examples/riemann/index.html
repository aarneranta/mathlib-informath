The Riemann hypothesis submission informalized

Levent Alpöge's Lean submission claiming proofs of the Riemann hypothesis and of the generalized Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions, as preserved in riemann-hypothesis-original (commit e4ed653). Its definitions and theorem statements are translated to Dedukti and read in English by Informath, with the symbol tables of mathlib-informath and of this example (riemann.dkgf). The proofs are not read. The submission has 175,187 modules and about a million declarations: these are 2522 declarations of the 49 modules at the top of the proof. See the comparison with the announcement: the claims, the statement the proof proves, and its last step.

Two modules of the notions are left out, because the exporter does not finish them: Development.AbelianVarietyTheory.Definitions.GlobalTwisting.APerTw (GlobalTheory.Fc.Hyps) and GlobalTwisting.DRS (GlobalTheory.Fc.DRS). They declare the 50-field structure of hypotheses that plugWin takes and one of its parameters.

The hypotheses of the last step

The proof derives EveryCharacter from three propositions, each proved elsewhere in the submission (CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean): Row0b of IsRecT (Galois representations attached to the regular algebraic polarized cusp representations of GL_a over CM fields, with local-global compatibility), RecTExists (a local Langlands correspondence with given laws at every place of every number field) and VWin (a bound on the lengths of the Tate-Shafarevich groups of the twists Q_l/Z_l(e) over subfields of cyclotomic fields), which the proof derives from Row0b.
