Mathlib informalized

Statements of Mathlib (commit 6972d52c0d), each with its Lean source, its translation to Dedukti by Export.lean, and its informalization by Informath, in English, as a formula, and in French, Czech, German and Swedish, which the buttons at each entry show there, and the check boxes above the entries everywhere. The Dedukti translation drops implicit and instance arguments and maps Lean's arithmetic and logic to Informath's base (mapping.tsv); the other Mathlib constants are worded by the symbol table mathlib.dkgf, whose English wordings Informath also renders in the other languages. Constants without a wording appear as $\mathrm{name}$.
