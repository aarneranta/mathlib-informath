Definition. Let $x \in \Natural$. Then $\mathrm{oddSum} (x)$ is a natural number.
By cases: \begin{itemize} \item $\mathrm{oddSum} (0)$ is $0$. \item for $n$, $\mathrm{oddSum}$ applied to the successor of $n$ is $\mathrm{oddSum} (n)+ (2 \times n + 1)$. \end{itemize}
Prime_two'. $2$ is prime.
Dvd_mul_self'. Let $a , b \in \Natural$ such that $a$ divides $b$. Then $a$ divides $b \times b$.
Mul_comm_group. Let $G$ be a commutative group. Let $a , b \in G$. Then $a \times b$ is equal to $b \times a$.
Cast_le'. Let $m , n \in \Natural$ such that $m \leq n$. Then $m \leq n$.
Mem_inter'. Let $X$ be a set. Let $s , t \in \wp X$. Let $x \in X$ such that $x$ is an element of $s$ and $x$ is an element of $t$. Then $x$ is an element of $\InterinterMACRoFC {s}{t}$.
Even_or_odd'. Let $n \in \Natural$. Then $n$ is even or odd.
